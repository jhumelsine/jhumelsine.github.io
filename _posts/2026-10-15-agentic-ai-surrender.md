I've struggled for months to make any meaningful progress with this blog entry.
I'm finding it difficult to construct a good narrative.

Write what you know, and since I have no practical or professional experience with AI, I'm not sure I have any meaningful credibility to discuss how it will shape software engineering.
The field is changing rapidly.
I know a change is coming.
I just don't know what that change will be or what form it will take.
I doubt that anyone knows how AI will shape software engineering, even those who are using AI practically and professionally.

I want to acknowledge Agentic AI, but I'm not the person who can tell the story.
Maybe in the future, I'll have something meaningful to add, but for now, I have to surrender and move on.

I'll continue this blog with topics for which I feel more qualified, such as Design Patterns.
However, I feel that the skills that I acquired in the past several decades are not as valuable as they once were.
I feel like I'm an expert buggy whip maker in the early days of the automobile.

Even if the topics I've blogged about are not as relevant as they once were, I'll still blog about them.
They may be useful when working with agents by directing them or to record how we used to develop software.
